# Official source discovery

## Source order

1. Use the official issuing authority's publication page or government legal-document system for the instrument's published text and metadata.
2. Use Vietnam's national legal database (`vbpl.vn`) to discover records, legal status, amendments, and links to official text. Confirm decisive clauses in the source instrument when possible.
3. Use the Ministry of Justice's Pháp điển portal (`phapdien.moj.gov.vn`) as a codified subject-matter view and navigation aid. Check the underlying instrument and amendment history when the answer depends on exact wording or dates.
4. Use an official gazette or other competent authority source when it is the publication record for the instrument in question.
5. Use secondary commentary, dataset cards, search results, or private mirrors only to locate a candidate source or explain a research lead. Do not treat them as sufficient proof of current legal effect.

The portal list is not exhaustive. Confirm the domain, authority, document identity, and content of each page during the task; do not assume that a familiar-looking page is official solely from its name.

## Search and verification steps

- Search by exact instrument number and title, then open the result and inspect its official record.
- Confirm issuer, document type, promulgation/publication date, and effective date against the text.
- Follow explicit amendment, replacement, repeal, and implementation references. Inspect commencement and transitional provisions, not just the amended article.
- For a historical question, establish which version was effective on the requested date. For a current question, state the date through which the status was checked.
- Preserve direct source URLs. Record access date when the source may change or be unavailable later.
- If the official portal is unavailable, say that primary-source verification could not be completed. Do not silently substitute a search snippet or a mirror.

## Source conflicts

Prefer the competent authority's published instrument over an unsourced summary. A codified or consolidated view can aid navigation, but if it disagrees with the amending instrument or its commencement clause, inspect the original materials and explain the discrepancy. If authoritative records remain inconsistent, abstain from a definite statement and identify the unresolved point.
